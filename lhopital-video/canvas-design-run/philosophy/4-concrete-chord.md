# Concrete Chord（混凝和弦）

A philosophy of pure structure singing — primary form and primary color arranged
with the rigor of a foundational course, where the square, the circle and the
triangle behave like notes struck together into a single resolved chord. Order
is the emotion; precision is the expression.

**Space and form.** A strict modular grid governs everything, yet the forms sit
within it with the poise of a sculpture. Perfect shapes — true circle, exact
square, equilateral triangle — are placed and re-placed until their spatial
tension resolves. Composition is architecture: load-bearing geometry, every
alignment the work of countless refinements.

**Color and material.** The primary triad — a confident red, a deep cobalt, a
warm yellow — on a paper-white field, flat and matte like printed gouache. Color
is information; each block is calibrated to hold its exact weight against the
others, with painstaking attention to balance, so the trio reads as a chord and
not a clash.

**Scale and rhythm.** Sculptural typography — a few characters set monumental and
exact — against tiny systematic labels and coordinate marks, as though the page
were a plate from a manual of form. The interplay of huge and minute creates a
measured, confident rhythm.

**Hierarchy.** Geometry leads, color defines, type anchors. Text is minimal and
structural — a title, a venue, a year — integrated into the grid as another
shape. The execution must be flawless and master-level: nothing overlapping,
every edge crisp, a composition that proves a discipline, ready for a museum of
modern design.
